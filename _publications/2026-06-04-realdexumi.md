Jiahui Huan is the **third author** of this work.

RealDexUMI is a wearable universal manipulation interface built around a shared dexterous end-effector module, in-hand vision, fingertip tactile sensing, and a palm-side isomorphic teleoperation glove. The paper reports an average success rate of 88.75% across eight real-robot tasks and evaluates transfer across three robot embodiments.
